Voices of Resistance & Justice

Exhibition Overview

This digital exhibition curates visual archives, solidarity posters, and biographical profiles documenting ongoing struggles for human rights, constitutional freedoms, political prisoners, and resistance against systemic discrimination. It serves as an archive of memory, dissent, and collective solidarity across academic campuses, grassroots movements, and international human rights causes.
