About

Fraternity Movement at Calicut University Campus is dedicated to advancing student fraternity, defending civil rights, and fighting for social equity across academic spaces. This platform serves as a curated archive of posters, political graphics, and public statements created for our campus movement. Click on any design to enlarge it.
